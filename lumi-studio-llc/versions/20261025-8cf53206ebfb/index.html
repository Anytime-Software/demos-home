  1. 01 Fine-line tattoo requests Fine-line tattoos use thin, precise lines for delicate, detailed designs. Tattoos
  2. 02 Tattoo touch-ups A touch-up refreshes a healed tattoo where lines or color have faded or settled unevenly. Tattoos
  3. 03 Tattoo parties A tattoo party is a booking for a small group who get tattooed during the same visit. Tattoos
  4. 04 Lash extensions/fills Lash extensions are individual lashes added to natural ones; fills replace those that have shed. More
  5. 05 Korean lash lift A lash lift curls natural lashes upward without extensions; Korean lash lift is one style of it. More
  6. 06 Lip blush Lip blush is cosmetic tattooing that adds soft, semi-permanent color to the lips. More
